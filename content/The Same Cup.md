---
title: The Same Cup
---

I am going to do this the only honest way I know. I am going to take the verses one at a time and pour myself onto each one until the thing I cannot hold shows itself, and I am not going to pretend I climbed out by the last line the way the nice essays do. I am going to leave myself where I actually am. You already know it is in me. Pretending otherwise is the worse lie.

> The LORD God said, "It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper suitable for him."
> Genesis 2:18

You looked at the one thing in all of creation You had not yet called good, and the not-good thing was a man by himself. So You made him a woman to end it. But You did not warn him. You did not tell him that the cure and the wound would wear the same face, that You were handing him the medicine and the poison in one cup and calling it good. I have been staring into that cup for a long time now, trying to understand why You would do that to us.

> Now to the unmarried and the widows I say: It is good for them to stay unmarried, as I do. But if they cannot control themselves, they should marry, for it is better to marry than to burn with passion.
> 1 Corinthians 7:8-9

And then You let Paul say this. Better to stay single, he says, I wish you were all like me, and two breaths from where You told me it is not good to be alone. You put both in the same book. You could have picked one. You gave me both and left me bleeding in the gap between them. So now marriage looks like a construction only a clown walks into with his eyes open, and the ones who stay out are not safe either, because half of them never even find the person. It is a wound if you win and a wound if you are never picked. A lottery on top of a knife.

> That is why a man leaves his father and mother and is united to his wife, and they become one flesh.
> Genesis 2:24

One flesh. I want that. I am not too proud to want it. But I am one. I am only one. I cannot make another person become one with me. I can pour and pour and she can stay two, stay a stranger, stay a country I am not allowed into, and there is nothing in my hands that changes it, because You gave her a will too and You will not override it, not for my sake, not even when it is killing me.

> Your desire will be for your husband, and he will rule over you.
> Genesis 3:16

And do not think I forgot what You did after we fell. You put the struggle into the marriage with Your own mouth. Before the fall it was one flesh, and after the fall it was a power fight, and You said it, You named it, You told us this ground would grow thorns and then You still send us out to farm it. So when I say marriage is dangerous I am not slandering it. I am reading You back to Yourself.

> When the LORD began to speak through Hosea, the LORD said to him, "Go, marry a promiscuous woman and have children with her."
> Hosea 1:2

I went looking in the prophets, thinking surely the men You chose got better wives, surely the ones You matched came out whole. And I found this. You told him to go and marry a whore. You knew she would break him. That was the point. You wanted a man walking around with a shattered marriage so the rest of us could see what You feel. You wounded Your own prophet on purpose.

> Go, show your love to your wife again, though she is loved by another man and is an adulteress. Love her as the LORD loves the Israelites.
> Hosea 3:1

And this is the part I cannot hold. You sent him back. You did not even let him keep the exit. You made him go back into the thing that cut him and love harder. If that is what You did to the man You chose, what exactly am I hoping for.

> not looking to your own interests but each of you to the interests of the others.
> Philippians 2:4

You tell me to give, but I am also told not to give her every want, because some of her wants would hurt her. Fine. But then I am the one deciding which of her wants are real and which I refuse, and now I am not a husband, I am a judge sitting over her, and how is that love. You never handed me the list. You just said seek her good and left me in the dark to guess which nights the good looks like a yes and which nights it looks like a no. I will get it wrong. I will hurt her either way.

> It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs.
> 1 Corinthians 13:5

Keeps no record of wrongs. And in the same breath I am told to know, to see clearly, whether I am in a marriage or being eaten alive. So which is it. Am I blind or am I awake. From where I sit those are the same act. To know she is destroying me I have to keep some kind of record, and to keep no record I have to let her destroy me and smile. You cannot have asked me for both. And yet there it is.

> My Father, if it is possible, may this cup be taken from me. Yet not as I will, but as you will.
> Matthew 26:39

The answer I keep getting handed is Him. He wanted the cup to pass and He drank it anyway.

> For the joy set before him he endured the cross, scorning its shame.
> Hebrews 12:2

Because of the joy set before Him. He traded the lesser want for the enormous one. That is beautiful and it is His and it is not mine, because I do not have the higher want. I have looked. There is no joy set before me that I can feel. There is just the cup. I have the lesser wants, the comfort, the peace, the wanting to be known, and nobody has put a great shining thing in front of me that makes those small enough to hand over. He surrendered a small thing for a thing He could see. You are asking me to surrender the small thing for nothing I can see, on the promise that the enormous thing is there in the dark where I cannot feel it, and to bet my one life on that.

> Love does not delight in evil but rejoices with the truth.
> 1 Corinthians 13:6

And I already tried. That is the part that shuts my mouth. I loved someone and I told her the truth, plainly, the way this line tells me to, and she did not change. Not once. I did the thing correctly and it broke on her like water on a stone. So I have no reason on earth to believe the next one is different, that anyone can clear their head enough to become one flesh with me, even if they wanted to, even if they were trying.

So here is where I actually am, and I am leaving myself here. I am the man alone that You called not good, holding the cure You gave that could be the poison, standing in front of a door that only a fool opens and only a fool refuses, with no higher want burning in me to make the trade easy, sure that whoever is on the other side will stay two while I am one, and remembering that even the wives You chose for Your own men left them bleeding.

The only thread I have is that when You told Hosea to go back, You were describing Yourself. You are the husband who married the unfaithful bride. You are the one who keeps loving what will not love You back. So maybe You never once said the marriage was safe. Maybe You are only telling me that this deadly thing, this cup that is medicine and poison at once, is the exact place You have been standing the whole time, holding the same cup, alone with a bride who will not come home.

That does not fix it. I want You to know that. It does not fix anything tonight. It is the only thing keeping me from putting the cup down for good, and I am telling You honestly it is a thin thread, and I am holding it in the dark, and I am tired.
