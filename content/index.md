title: Mimesis & the Cross
---

Essays reading the Gospels the way René Girard read them: as forensics, not sermons. The claim is simple and testable. Human violence runs on a hidden mechanism, all-against-one, and the Bible is the one ancient text that turns the lights on in the room where the mechanism works.

Start here:

- [[The Commandment You Cannot Obey]] — the tenth commandment discovers mimetic desire, hits the wall no law can climb, and needs a Person to finish the sentence.
- [[The Crucifixion Satan Never Expected]] — the cross as a victim mechanism like all the others, with one detail that breaks it.
