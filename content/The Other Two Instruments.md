*Part 11 of eleven. The series is [[Something That Can Say No]].*

Two other studies ran alongside the scapegoat detector. Different subjects, same posture, and one of them is better designed than anything in the main track.

## Counting the contradictions

The question: when somebody says the New Testament contradicts itself, how many of the alleged contradictions survive contact with a rule fixed in advance?

The docket had to come from somewhere hostile or the study is worthless. So it came from an aggregation of the Skeptic's Annotated Bible, Infidels.org and EvilBible. Which means I could not skip the hard ones.

That structural protection is doing all the work. It makes the result a **ceiling on the accusers' case**, never a floor under anybody's faith.

A contradiction was defined before any item was scored. Two texts contradict when they assert P and not-P about the same subject, in the same respect, at the same time, and no licensed reading makes both true.

Nine gates handle the ways an allegation can fail to be one. A surviving clash is then priced from 1 to 4, where 4 means no rescue exists at all.

## And the falsifier fired

**F4 fired.** Two of the nine gates, A2 and A5, accounted for **67.6% of all rejections**, against a ceiling of 40% declared in advance.

Concentration like that means the gates are doing more work than a rule should. So **the dissolution rate is reported unreliable.**

That is the headline, at the top, before anything that survives.

Then the survival argument, which has to be structural rather than an assertion that it is fine. **An A2 error returns an item at cost 1 or 2. It cannot manufacture a cost 3.** So push the worst case as far as it goes.

| | as scored | worst case |
|---|---|---|
| admitted | 51 | **120** (78.4% of all entries) |
| at cost 3 | 2 | **2** |
| at cost 4 | 0 | **0** |

Admit every single rejected item and the sharp end of a 210-item accusation is still two items.

**And it costs something.** Judas at cost 3 means one of Matthew and Acts is wrong about a fact. That is flatly incompatible with inerrancy, and this study says so rather than working around it.

The Judas item was revised from cost 4 down to cost 3 during the work. Against the rhetorically stronger position. Cost 4 means no rescue exists, and an ad hoc one does.

Three findings are judgement-free and anybody can check them.

**Fifty-seven of 210 entries, 27%, are the same complaint recounted.** Five allegations misdescribe their own citations. Four put a question on one side of the contradiction, which is not an assertion and therefore cannot contradict anything.

## The study that was designed best and nearly never ran

There is a fourth pre-registration in this repository that sat unrun for weeks.

It is the best-designed file in the whole project. A control. A denominator. Five falsifiers with numbers fixed in advance. Six named confounds, including an admission of possible circularity. And an order of operations requiring the **control corpus to be coded first**.

The question: do the canonical resurrection narratives show the seven features that critics' own models of legendary growth predict? Against Gospel of Peter and Acts of Pilate as a control.

Twenty-two units, thirteen canonical and nine apocryphal.

**H3 fired.** The gap between the two sets is **0.225** against a threshold of 0.25. Direction without magnitude.

**H5 fired.** Leave-one-out was run before the headline ratio, as the pre-registration required. **Four of the 22 units move the verdict on their own.**

**H2 did not fire**, and that is the important negative. A feature set that cannot detect legendary development in the *Acts of Pilate* cannot detect its absence anywhere. So if the control had scored under 0.50, the instrument was invalid.

The control came in at **0.577.** The instrument works.

And one feature is worth arguing in public.

| | canon | control |
|---|---|---|
| **E1, the resurrection directly narrated** | **0 / 5 = 0.00** | **3 / 5 = 0.60** |

**Not one canonical text narrates the resurrection itself.** Matthew stops short: the angel rolls the stone away and the tomb is already empty. The *Gospel of Peter* opens the heavens, brings three men out, and has a cross walk out behind them and answer a question.

It is categorical, and it survives every leave-one-out.

**And E6 runs backwards, hard.** The canonical texts resolve the followers' doubt far *more* than the apocrypha do, 0.73 against 0.25. Thomas, Emmaus, the broiled fish. Peter leaves the women fleeing.

That is a result against the hypothesis and it is not small.

A confound named in advance called the mechanism correctly. E6 is applicable eleven times in the canon and four times in the control, so it is measuring subject matter rather than development.

So the general claim gets dropped. *The canonical accounts are strikingly restrained* is not supportable. Three of seven features carry it and one runs the other way. E1 survives, on its own, stated narrowly.

## The instrument that measured the wrong object

The third study takes the conversion Girard describes at the end of *Deceit, Desire and the Novel* and scores it on the last 1,500 words of novel endings.

Two problems, and I found the second one only by failing.

**The first is a selection nobody declared.** Girard names **seven** transformations on that page. Deception into truth, anguish into remembrance, agitation into repose, hatred into love, humiliation into humility, mediated desire into autonomy, deviated into vertical transcendency. The instrument scores **three**, and the choice of which three was never written down anywhere.

**The second is worse and better.** The fetch that was supposed to build the control set was killed partway through. **Set B never existed and the two primary predictions never ran.** No control. No Dostoevsky, no Proust.

What did run failed. P1 predicted the conversion bits would appear in 70% of Girard's own canon and got **50%**.

Then the pre-registered case, and it is the useful one. *The Bell Jar* was predicted in advance, in conversation, before the rubric existed, to score T = 1, R = 0, L = 0. It scored **0, 0, 0.**

R and L came out as predicted, with the exact quotes the prediction was built on. T failed, and the reason is precise. Esther names the mechanism, quotably, *"for the price of their care and influence, have me resemble them"*, in **chapter XVIII**, roughly nine thousand words before the end.

The window cannot see it. And widening the window to reach it is tuning an instrument to make a text pass, so it stays unfixed and the failure stays in the record.

Then *Madame Bovary* scored zero, and this is the finding worth having.

Girard classes *Bovary* as **novelistic**, the highest category he has. Its ending scores the same as a novel he would call romantic. The reason is visible in the text: **the lucidity in Flaubert belongs to the narration, not to any character.** Nobody in that book ever sees the triangle. The author does, continuously, and hands it to the reader over the characters' heads.

> **So the three pairs describe what happens to a character, while *novelistic* names where the author is standing.** The instrument, applied honestly, measures the wrong object, and it took a failing score on Flaubert to show it.

That is the best thing this instrument produced, and it is a conceptual finding delivered by a failed prediction rather than a passing one.

## The defect all three studies share

Put the three side by side and one thing is common to all of them, and it is not a technique.

The scapegoat detector has no control group. It compares tiers of texts against each other and against shuffled versions of themselves, which tells you about the texts and never about the instrument.

The conversion instrument's control set was never built, so its two primary predictions never ran. What is left is four texts and no comparison.

The restraint control **has** one, and it is the only one of the three that can interpret its own numbers.

Watch how much that buys. The canonical set scored 0.352. On its own that number means nothing whatever. Is 0.352 low? Low compared to what? It is only a low score because the *Acts of Pilate* and the *Gospel of Peter*, coded first, on the same seven features, by the same person, came in at 0.577.

> **Without the control, 0.352 is a number. With it, 0.352 is a finding.**

And the control does a second job that is easy to miss. It tests the instrument. H2 exists precisely so that a feature set which cannot detect legendary development anywhere gets caught before it is pointed at the canon. Had the apocrypha scored 0.4, the study would have ended there, and correctly.

Nothing in the main track has that. Ten rounds of the scapegoat detector produced no moment at which the instrument could have been declared invalid by its own evidence.

Two positive results survive.

In the four Gospels, crowd-division language sits nearer to violence language than chance places it, at p = 0.0015. And persecution-shaped texts contain persecution vocabulary more than frequency-matched random vocabulary does, at p = 0.0045. The pre-registration itself called that second one **the uncontroversial half** of Girard's claim, before the number existed.

The second of those cannot be reproduced, because its script was never saved.

That is the yield. And a reader who has followed this far should notice that the yield is not what the series was for.

## The sentence worth keeping

The thing this project actually produced is not a result about Girard. It is a set of findings about what it takes to build something that can tell you no, and one of them outranks the rest.

The scapegoat detector spent ten rounds getting more refined. Better lexicon, better nulls, better statistics, better falsifiers. It never had a control group.

The restraint control is a cruder instrument by every technical measure. Seven binary features, coded by hand, on 22 units. It has a control corpus, and because it does, it can say what its own numbers mean. H2 not firing is the moment that study earned the right to be believed, and nothing in ten rounds of the main track has an equivalent.

> **A cruder instrument with a control beats a refined instrument without one.**

Ten rounds of the main track are ten rounds of refining an instrument without one.

And the refrain, one last time, because every instance in this series was found in the same place. Not in the hypothesis, which was doubted from the first commit. In the apparatus built to do the doubting.

> **A check that cannot fail prints reassurance.**

Everything here is on disk. The repository, the pre-registrations, the commit order, the corpus, the scripts, the failures. Go and check it, and if something in it is wrong, the record was built so that you can show me.
